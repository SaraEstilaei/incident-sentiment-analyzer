Soft-voting ensemble: how the two models are combined

Select the fusion weight by validation log-loss, never by looking at test labels.

VALIDATION-SELECTED WEIGHT

Weighted soft voting

Pensemble = w × PDeBERTa + (1 − w) × PNLPTown
  • Generate a probability vector across five star classes
  • Sweep DeBERTa weights from 0.0 to 1.0 in 0.1 increments
  • Choose w = 0.7 using validation log-loss (0.6356)
  • Apply the selected 70/30 mix to the final test and submission
